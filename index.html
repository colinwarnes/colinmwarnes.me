Colin M. Warnes, MPH

PhD Candidate, Imperial College London

cmw23@ic.ac.uk

I'm a doctoral student in the Dorigatti Group at Imperial College London. My work sits between computational modelling and the lab: I use statistical and within-host models to profile viral biomarker kinetics across the course of infection, with a focus on how acute-stage biomarkers can inform interventions. Before Imperial I worked on dengue, chikungunya and yellow fever at UC Berkeley and spent two years as a molecular biologist in biotech.
